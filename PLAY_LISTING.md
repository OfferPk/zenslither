## Full Description (Max 4000 chars)
Immerse your mind in the serene elegance of ZenSlither, a meditative and deeply satisfying Slitherlink loop logic puzzle game designed for pure relaxation and mental clarity.

Inspired by traditional Japanese paper-and-pencil logic puzzles, ZenSlither invites you into a peaceful woodland garden of dots and numbers. Your objective is simple yet deeply engaging: connect the lattice pins with horizontal and vertical lines to weave a single continuous, non-intersecting loop guided by gentle numeric clues.

### How to Play
1. An $N \times N$ lattice grid is populated with pins and numeric clues (0, 1, 2, or 3).
2. The number inside any cell tells you exactly how many of its 4 surrounding edges are part of the loop.
3. Empty cells without numbers can hold any number of edges.
4. All lines must connect to form ONE single unbroken closed loop.
5. Every pin touched by the loop must connect exactly two edges—no branches, no dead ends, no separate loops!

### Serene Japanese Acoustics
Experience pure acoustic mindfulness powered by our built-in procedural Web Audio synthesizer. Hear the rich, resonant pluck of a traditional Japanese silk koto string with every line you place, the crisp tap of dry bamboo on cross marks, and a celebratory pentatonic victory arpeggio that rings out in harmony whenever you close the loop.

### Thoughtful, Player-Centric Features
- **50 Handcrafted Levels**: Progress from gentle 5x5 meadow grids to intricate 9x9 mystical labyrinths across 5 themed packs.
- **100% Uniquely Solvable**: Every single level is mathematically verified by our constraint deduction solver. No guessing or trial-and-error required—pure logic alone will guide you to the solution.
- **Auto-Cross Helper**: Optionally auto-cross incident edges when a pin reaches degree 2, letting you focus on higher-level path deduction.
- **Smart Logic Deduction Hints**: Stuck on a tricky section? The hint assistant doesn't just give away the answer; it explains the logic behind the next move with animated edge highlights.
- **Unlimited Undo & Restart**: Experiment freely with full undo history.
- **100% Offline & Private**: Zero internet connection needed, zero accounts, zero analytics, zero ads, zero battery-draining bloatware.

### Progressive Level Packs
- 🌿 **Meadow Loop** (5×5): Ideal for beginners to learn core corner and neighbor deductions.
- 🍃 **Willow Winding** (6×6): Introduces diagonal parity and loop routing techniques.
- 🎋 **Bamboo Lattice** (7×7): Deeper tactical deductions with alternating clue chains.
- 🌲 **Forest Fen** (8×8): Expansive grids requiring global connectivity vision.
- 🌌 **Mystic Maze** (9×9): Masterful multi-step loop challenges for true logic enthusiasts.

Find your rhythm, unwind your thoughts, and weave the golden loop in ZenSlither!
